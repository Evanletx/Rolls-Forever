Rolls Forever

Find out if you really never win anything.

Introduction How it works Install Commands Notes License Credits

Introduction

I don't know about you, but I haven't won anything since 2006.
Rolls Forever lets you find out if that's actually true.

This mod automagically keeps a record of every Need and Greed roll you make in WoW Forever, on every character.

How it works

Each time an item comes up for a roll, Rolls Forever notes the item, whether you picked Need, Greed or Pass, the number you rolled, who won and what they rolled.

View rolls per character, and you can switch between or look at all of them together.

Open it with /rolls, the minimap button, or the addon menu on the minimap.

Tabs

All Rolls, Need and Greed.

Character dropdown

Each character on its own, or an All Characters view.

Calendar filter

Pick a date and look back 30 days, 60 days, 3, 6 or 9 months, a year, or all time.

Search box

Find an item, a winner or a location by name.

Stats panel

Median, highest and lowest roll, natural 100s and 1s, luck, win and dry streaks, closest loss, and where you roll the most.

Install

  1. Download or clone the repository.
  2. Put the folder in your WoW Forever addons folder, named RollsForever: 
    World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/RollsForever
  3. Restart the game (or /reload) and make sure Rolls Forever is ticked in the AddOns list.

Commands

/rollsOpens or closes the window. /rf and /rollsforever work too.
/rolls scale 0.9Makes the window smaller or bigger, anywhere from 0.6 to 1.5.
/rolls minimapShows or hides the minimap button.
/rolls resetWipes the saved rolls for the character you're on. Asks you to type /rolls reset confirm first.
/rolls aboutVersion, credits and where to get help.
